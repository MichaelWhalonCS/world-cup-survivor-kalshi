▶ How World Cup Survivor Pools Work & How to Read This Table

Format

  • 48 nations in 12 groups of 4 (new expanded format). Each team plays 3 group matches.
  • Top 2 from each group + 8 best 3rd-place finishers advance to the Round of 32, then knockout to the Final.
  • Group matches can end in a draw — only outright wins help your survivor pick.

How Survivor Pools Work (World Cup variant)

  • Group stage — pick one nation per matchday whose game you think they'll win outright. A draw is treated as a loss for survivor purposes.
  • Knockout — pick one nation per round who you think will win their game (after 90/120 mins or penalties).
  • Each nation may only be used once over the entire tournament. Save your top seeds for the rounds where you actually need them.

Data Sources

  • Kalshi per-game markets — for each group match, Kalshi lists separate "Team A wins" / "Team B wins" / "Draw" contracts. The MD1/MD2/MD3 columns show the last-traded YES price on the team-wins contract.
  • Kalshi advancement futures — "Will Brazil reach the Quarterfinals?", etc. The Make R32, Make R16, Make QF, Make SF, Make Final columns are the implied cumulative probability of reaching that stage. "Make R32" = qualifying from the group (first knockout round of the 48-team format).
  • Champion futures — Kalshi's "winner of the 2026 World Cup" event.

How the Numbers Are Calculated

  • Cumulative probabilities (the KO round columns) come straight from advancement futures.
  • Conditional (game-win) probabilities are derived by dividing consecutive rounds: P(win R16 game) = P(reach QF) / P(reach R16).
  • Group-stage probabilities are per-game team-win prices (the MD1/MD2/MD3 columns); they are not cumulative because every team plays all 3 matchdays regardless of earlier results.
  • Best Pick = the latest round where this team's per-game win probability is ≥ 65%. (Lower threshold than basketball because draws compress per-game soccer win rates.)

Strategy Tips

  • Group-stage win rates are lower than basketball — a top seed against a Pot 4 opponent might only price at 65–75% because of the draw possibility.
  • Don't waste a Pot 1 nation on MD1 if their MD3 opponent is much weaker — check all three matchdays in the row.
  • R32 and R16 are the "easy" knockout rounds for top seeds. QF onwards is where favourites get squeezed.
